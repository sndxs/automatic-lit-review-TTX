\section{Methodology}

This report summarizes findings from an automated, country-by-country news search (\texttt{deep\_dive\_news.py}), separate from this project's main academic literature pipeline. For each country, search terms are translated into that country's dominant language via the Claude API, GDELT's DOC~2.0 API is queried for candidate articles from that country's press, and candidates are fetched and screened for genuine relevance (not generic use of the word ``test'') by the same model, which also translates and summarizes anything relevant into English. As of this report, 189 of 189 countries have been searched (harvested), 7 candidate articles have been read and judged, and \textbf{3 were found genuinely relevant}, spanning 2 countries.

\subsection{United Kingdom}

\textbf{Compliance and access dominate university assessment plans, confirms testing giant} \citep{thepienewscom2026n0} (English). \subsection{United States}

\textbf{Do Florida's record-high school grades mask concerns about student achievement?} \citep{wlrnorg2026n1} (English). This WLRN article examines Florida's school grading system, which is based partly on the Florida Assessment of Student Thinking (FAST) standardized test results. It questions whether record numbers of A and B grades mask the reality that students are falling behind nationally in reading and math, raising concerns among parents and educators about whether state testing standards truly reflect learning.

\textbf{The graduate admissions exam grind} \citep{cavalierdailycom2026n2} (English). The Cavalier Daily examines how University of Virginia students face renewed standardized testing pressure with graduate school entrance exams (like GRE), after already dealing with such exams in high school. It explores the stress and preparation involved in these admissions tests.
